The Problem

A guaranteed-attention channel, treated like paperwork.

Banks spend heavily on paid media to buy attention they already have for free. Mandatory communications get opened out of necessity — but arrive generic, static, and disconnected from any engagement strategy. Closing that gap is an operating-model question, not a template question: it changes who owns the send, and which line of the P&L it lands on.

Operating Model

Who owns this channel, and which line it lands on.

Personalizing the envelope is the visible part. What makes this a digital transformation rather than a product feature is where the channel sits afterwards: who owns the send, how it is funded, and what it is measured on.

Dimension Today With Kairos
Who owns the send Compliance and Operations. Marketing has no route into the message. Joint. Compliance owns the mandated fields and keeps a veto; Marketing owns the envelope around them.
How it is budgeted A compliance cost line, measured on cost per message and delivery SLA. An attributed revenue channel with its own P&L, carried at near-zero acquisition cost.
What success means Sent, delivered, no complaint raised. Delivery SLA held and next-best-action uptake, cross-sell conversion, and trust measured per cycle.
Speed of change Any template change queues behind a release cycle. The envelope adapts per customer, per cycle. The mandated layer stays version-controlled and auditable.
Risk posture Risk avoided by never touching the message. Risk managed: consent and eligibility checks before send, model governance, and an audit trail on every variant.

Journey Orchestration Assist

The marketer still decides. AI just stops starting from a blank canvas.

Instead of a business user building customer journeys from scratch in the CRM, AI proposes two or three ready-made journeys from existing customer data. The human picks one, edits the steps, approves it, or throws it away — judgment stays human, the blank page doesn't. Try it: select a journey, then reorder, reword, or remove a step.

Roadmap

Where this starts, and where it goes.

01
Statement & bill personalization pilotthis demo
Launch on one card portfolio and one channel. Pareto dashboard, insight, and one cross-sell action layered on the existing statement — no change to mandated content. Stand up the content contract with Compliance and the attribution needed to measure the channel at all.
02
Expand alerts + journey orchestration assist
Extend personalization to EMI reminders, KYC alerts, and renewal notices, and introduce AI-suggested journeys into the CRM for marketers to select and refine. Ownership of the envelope moves to a joint Marketing–Compliance model, with model governance and consent checks in the send path.
03
Full attribution & channel reclassification
Measure cross-sell conversion and trust impact end-to-end and extend the same engine across SMS, app, and email. Mandatory communications move off the compliance cost line and onto an attributed revenue channel carrying its own P&L — the point at which this stops being a feature and becomes an operating-model change.
